Screen Problems Self-Check

This tool sorts screen flicker, stripes and corruption, external monitor detection, wrong colors, the refresh rate and a black screen after signing in into a few questions. Each step shows what you need and how to do it, and if it is too hard to do yourself, it tells you what to do next.

🔒 Runs only in your browser · nothing is sent to a server

Pick a symptom

  • The screen flickers or shakes
  • Stripes or broken color blocks appear
  • An external or dual monitor does not show
  • The screen colors look wrong
  • A black screen after signing in
  • The graphics driver stopped responding

The question-by-question check needs JavaScript to be turned on.

What this tool does and does not do

  • What it does: sorts the symptom and guides you step by step through the easy checks first: cable, settings and driver. Steps that involve touching the graphics card show what you need and the cautions first.
  • What it does not do: it cannot confirm a faulty graphics card or monitor. Confirming that takes a test with another monitor or graphics card, or an on-site check.
  • What it does not cover: a PC that switches on but shows no picture at all is covered by the "PC won't start" self-check.

Frequently asked questions

How do I tell whether the flicker is the monitor or the computer?

Connect the same monitor to another PC, or connect another monitor to this PC. If the symptom follows the monitor when you change it, it is likely the monitor; if it stays on the PC, it is likely the PC (cable, graphics card or driver).

Isn't it risky to remove the graphics driver?

Even if you remove the driver, Windows runs with a basic driver and the screen still shows. It is safer to download the latest driver from the maker's site beforehand and then go ahead.
