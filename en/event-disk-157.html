    EVENT ID 157

    Disk Event 157: Disk Was Surprise Removed

    157 records that the operating system detected a disk being removed without a shutdown procedure. In most cases an external drive was unplugged without safe removal, or the connection dropped for a moment.

    If it appears on an internal drive, safe removal is irrelevant; check the power supply, a poor SATA or M.2 contact, and whether a flat battery forced a shutdown.

    When it appears

    • An external drive was suddenly disconnected
    • Power was cut while a USB device was connected
    • A laptop was forced off by a flat battery

    Likely causes

    • The device was removed without safe removal
    • A poor USB cable contact
    • A flat battery or a power error

    What to check, in order

    1. Always use safe removal for external devices. Start with what is easy to undo so you can narrow the range without risk.
    2. Check the state of the cable and the port. Compare with the first check to narrow the cause candidates.
    3. Check the file system for damage (chkdsk). Use this to verify the next cause when the earlier steps did not solve it.
    Caution

    A forced removal may have damaged files. Check the important files first.

    Frequently asked questions

    • Can 157 remain even though I used safe removal? Normally it does not remain if you went through the safe-removal procedure. If it does, the connection may already have dropped during removal, or the device was unplugged before the procedure finished.
    • What should I check if it repeats on an internal drive? If it is not an external device, suspect unstable power delivery or a poor SATA or M.2 slot contact. Check the cable and power connectors first.
