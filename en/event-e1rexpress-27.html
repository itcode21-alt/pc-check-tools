    EVENT ID 27

    e1rexpress Event 27: Intel Wired Link Warning

    e1rexpress 27 records that an Intel wired network adapter lost its link or warned about the connection state. It is a separate driver record with a different source from e2fexpress 27, which has the same number.

    Change the cable, the wall jack, the switch port and the driver one by one and check. If it reproduces when you move the cable, a contact problem is likely. First compare whether the time of the actual drop matches the time of the record.

    When it appears

    • The internet drops briefly and recovers
    • The wired connection drops during a game or file transfer
    • It reproduces when the cable is moved

    Likely causes

    • Contact problems in the LAN cable, wall jack or switch port
    • Ethernet driver, power management or speed negotiation problems
    • Link instability of the router, switch or the equipment on the other end

    What to check, in order

    1. Write down the link state and adapter name on the General tab and in the XML.
    2. Cross-check with another cable, port and network.
    3. Check the adapter's power management and the speed and duplex settings at their defaults.
    4. Compare whether the time overlaps with 4199, 4266 and 1014.
    Caution

    The source differs from e2fexpress 27. Check the time of the error together with the actual drop before deciding to replace the adapter.

    Frequently asked questions

    • Are e1rexpress and e2fexpress the same? Both are Intel wired LAN driver families, but the source (driver name) differs. It can look different depending on the adapter you use, so check the source name first.
    • Is changing the LAN cable enough? Replacing the cable is the easiest first step, but also check the switch port, the adapter's power management and the speed and duplex settings.
