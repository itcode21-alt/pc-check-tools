    EVENT ID 161

    Volmgr Event 161: Crash Dump Could Not Be Saved

    161 records that saving a crash dump after a blue screen or system halt failed. More often than not it explains why no dump was left for analysis, rather than being the cause of the halt.

    If no dump is left, there is nothing for a minidump analyser to read, so first check the page-file settings and the free space on the system drive. Viewing it together with BugCheck 1001, Kernel-Power 41 and WHEA records from the same time shows the flow.

    When it appears

    • No minidump is created after a blue screen
    • Recorded just before Kernel-Power 41
    • A message saying dump-file creation failed is shown

    Likely causes

    • The page file is missing or is not large enough to save a dump
    • Not enough free space on the system drive where the dump is saved
    • A storage-device error or failed access to the boot disk
    • The dump write did not finish because of an unexpected power cut

    What to check, in order

    1. Check whether C:\Windows\Minidump and C:\Windows\MEMORY.DMP were created.
    2. In System Properties → Startup and Recovery, check the debugging-information setting and the page-file setting.
    3. Compare the free space on the system drive with Disk, Ntfs and storport events.
    4. Check BugCheck 1001, Kernel-Power 41 and WHEA records from the same time.
    5. If a dump was created, check the faulting driver separately with a minidump analyser.
    Caution

    Do not conclude from 161 alone that the GPU, PSU or storage device has failed. Separate the failure to create a dump from the real cause of the halt.

    If storage-device errors are present too, back up important files before changing the dump settings.

    Frequently asked questions

    • If 161 is present, has the SSD failed? You cannot conclude that. It is left when the page file is missing or short of space, and also when the power is suddenly cut and the write does not finish. Check whether storage-device events (Disk, Ntfs) are present with it.
    • What should I check to get a dump left? Check the debugging-information setting and the page-file setting in System Properties → Startup and Recovery, and free up space on the system drive.
