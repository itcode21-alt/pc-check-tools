INPUT CHECK

Input & Sound Self-Check

This tool sorts keyboard and mouse detection, single keys that fail, the laptop touchpad, sound, sound from only one earphone, the microphone, the webcam, Bluetooth and USB detection into questions. Each step shows what you need and how to do it, and if it is too hard to do yourself, it tells you what to do next.

🔒 Runs only in your browser · nothing is sent to a server

Pick a symptom

  • A keyboard or mouse is not detected
  • Only certain keyboard keys do not press
  • The laptop touchpad does not work
  • There is no sound
  • The microphone is not picked up
  • A Bluetooth device cannot be found
  • A USB device is not detected

The question-by-question check needs JavaScript to be turned on.

What this tool does and does not do

  • What it does: guides you step by step, starting with the easy checks that tell whether the device itself or the PC is the problem: ports, settings and drivers.
  • What it does not do: it cannot confirm a fault in the internal circuit. A laptop keyboard or touchpad has to be taken apart, so it is hard to repair yourself.
  • What it does not cover: printer and gamepad problems.

Frequently asked questions

What if only the front USB or front audio jack does not work?

First test by plugging into the ports wired directly to the motherboard on the back of the PC (the back USB and audio jacks). If it works on the back, the front panel cable or its connection is the likely cause.

What is the first thing to try when a wireless device does not work?

Replace the battery with a new one, plug the receiver into another USB port and pair the device again. It is the easiest and most common fix.
