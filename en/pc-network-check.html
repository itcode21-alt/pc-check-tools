NETWORK CHECK

Internet & Network Self-Check

This tool sorts the internet not working at all, Wi-Fi dropping, only certain sites not opening, a refused-connection error, the network dropping after an update, game connection errors and shared folder problems into questions. Each step shows what you need and how to do it, and if it is too hard to do yourself, it tells you what to do next.

🔒 Runs only in your browser · nothing is sent to a server

Pick a symptom

  • The internet keeps dropping
  • Wi-Fi drops or the signal is weak
  • Only certain sites won't open
  • A "refused to connect" error
  • The internet stopped working after an update
  • I can't connect to a game server

The question-by-question check needs JavaScript to be turned on.

What this tool does and does not do

  • What it does: guides you step by step through checks that tell where the problem is: another device on the same router, another network (a phone hotspot), and wired versus wireless.
  • What it does not do: it cannot confirm an internet provider line outage or a broken router. A line problem has to be checked with your internet provider's customer service.
  • What it does not cover: detailed router settings (port forwarding and so on) and company network policy problems.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Command Prompt commands (ipconfig, netsh) safe?

ipconfig /release, /renew and /flushdns get a new IP and a fresh DNS cache, and netsh winsock reset returns the network settings to their defaults. They are reversible settings resets, but on a company PC, ask the administrator first.

Does restarting the router erase its settings?

Just switching the power off and on does not erase the settings. It is different from holding down the Reset button. Do not press the Reset button.
