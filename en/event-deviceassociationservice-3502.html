    EVENT ID 3502

    DeviceAssociationService Event 3502: Device Unpairing Failed

    DeviceAssociationService 3502 is an error-level record that Windows tried to disconnect (unpair) a device and failed. For example, if AepId starts with EsclUsb and ClientProcessName is spoolsv.exe, the print spooler could not clean up a USB-connected printer or scanner.

    It often repeats at every boot because Windows tries to clean up connection information left after the device was already unplugged, and it sometimes has no effect on actual use. In Settings, remove the devices you no longer use under Printers & scanners and reinstall the driver.

    When it appears

    • When a device such as a printer or scanner is removed or its connection drops
    • Booting with a USB printer unplugged

    Likely causes

    • Windows tries again to unpair a device that no longer exists
    • The device driver or print spooler could not clean up the connection information

    What to check, in order

    1. Identify the device from AepId and ClientProcessName (for example spoolsv.exe).
    2. Remove the leftover device in Settings > Printers & scanners.
    3. Check whether that device actually has a symptom of not working.
    Caution

    If the device works normally, it is usually not urgent. If it still repeats after removing it, uninstall the printer driver and reinstall it.

    Frequently asked questions

    • 3502 is left at every boot — is that okay? If the device works normally, it is usually fine. It is most likely a record of Windows failing to clean up a device that is already gone.
    • How can I get rid of it? Identify the device from AepId and ClientProcessName, remove that device in Settings > Printers & scanners, and restart. If it keeps being left, uninstall the printer or scanner driver and install the latest version again.
