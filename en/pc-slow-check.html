SLOW & NOISY PC CHECK

Slow, Hot & Noisy PC Self-Check

This tool sorts general slowness, slow boot-up, fan noise, shutdowns from overheating, hard drive noise, graphics card high-pitched noise and frame rate drops in games into questions. Each step shows what you need and how to do it, and if it is too hard to do yourself, it tells you what to do next.

🔒 Runs only in your browser · nothing is sent to a server

Pick a symptom

  • The computer has become slow overall
  • Booting takes a long time
  • The fan got louder
  • It is hot and suddenly shuts off
  • The hard drive clicks
  • The frame rate drops in games

The question-by-question check needs JavaScript to be turned on.

What this tool does and does not do

  • What it does: guides you step by step through checks that tell the cause: what is high in Task Manager, the temperature and dust, and the condition of the storage device.
  • What it does not do: it cannot confirm a part failure. Checks that need taking the PC apart, such as replacing thermal grease or the cooler, are left to a professional rather than done yourself.
  • What it does not cover: a PC that does not switch on is covered by the "PC won't start" self-check.

Frequently asked questions

Task Manager shows the disk at 100%. Is it broken?

No. It may be a temporary task such as a Windows update or search indexing, or a shortage of storage space. First check whether it lasts for more than a few minutes and which program is using it.

Can I keep using a hard drive that clicks?

If a regular clicking repeats, the longer you keep using it the more the data can be damaged. Back up the important data first, and if the sound is severe, switch the power off and ask for help.
