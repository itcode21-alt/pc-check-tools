PC WON'T TURN ON CHECK

PC Won't Turn On Self-Check

This tool sorts a PC that won't turn on, fans that spin with no display, a computer that starts and shuts off, a blue screen or a stuck sign-in screen, and laptop charging and sleep problems into questions. Each step shows what you need and how to do it, and if it is too hard to do yourself, it tells you what to do next.

🔒 Runs only in your browser · nothing is sent to a server

Pick a symptom

  • PC won't turn on at all (no fans, no lights)
  • Fans spin but no display (no signal)
  • It turns on, then shuts off or keeps restarting
  • Stuck on boot, blue screen or the sign-in screen
  • Laptop not charging or won't wake from sleep

The question-by-question check needs JavaScript to be turned on.

What this tool does and does not do

  • What it does: guides you step by step through the easy checks first (power cable, switches, connections) and then the ones that tell the cause, such as memory, graphics card and power supply.
  • What it does not do: it cannot confirm a part failure. Checks that need the case or the laptop opened up are marked as such, and the ones with a risk of damage are left to a professional.
  • What it does not cover: for a PC that runs but is slow or hot, use the slow & noisy PC self-check; for a screen that shows odd pictures, use the screen self-check.

Frequently asked questions

My PC won't turn on and nothing happens. Is the power supply dead?

Not necessarily. A loose power cable, a switched-off PSU switch, or a power button wire that has come loose can look the same. Check the outlet, the switch on the back of the PSU and the front panel power switch wire before you replace anything.

The fans spin and the lights come on but there is no display. What does that mean?

This is often called "no POST" or "no display". It is commonly a monitor cable plugged into the motherboard instead of the graphics card, memory that is not fully seated, or a graphics card problem. The self-check goes through these in order.

What if I can't find my BitLocker recovery key?

Without the key the data cannot be unlocked. Check your Microsoft account (aka.ms/myrecoverykey), your work or school account, and any printout or USB stick. Do not reset or reinstall before you have asked for help.
