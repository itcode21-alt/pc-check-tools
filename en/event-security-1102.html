    EVENT ID 1102

    Security Event 1102: The Audit Log Was Cleared

    1102 records that the security audit log was cleared. An administrator may have tidied the log on purpose, but it can also appear when someone tries to erase traces after a compromise, so you must find who cleared it and when.

    If this event appears and neither you nor your IT staff remember clearing the log, compare the sign-in records and the traces of programs that ran just before the clearing in other logs, such as the System log.

    When it appears

    • After the Security event log was reset
    • When malware infection is suspected

    Likely causes

    • Intentional log clearing by an administrator
    • Log clearing by malicious software

    What to check, in order

    1. Check the account name and time of the person who cleared the log.
    2. Compare unusual sign-ins or program executions before and after the clearing.
    3. If the clearing was not intended, carry out a security check.
    Caution

    An unintended clearing of the security log can be a sign of a security incident.

    Frequently asked questions

    • Does 1102 always mean I was hacked? No. It is also left by a normal, administrative log cleanup. If you do not remember clearing it, check whether the account name and time match your or your IT staff's work.
    • Can the earlier records be restored once the log is cleared? By default, a cleared event log is hard to recover. Exporting logs regularly to files lets you check earlier records even in such a case.
