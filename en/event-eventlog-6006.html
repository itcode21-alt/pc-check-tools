    EVENT ID 6006

    EventLog Event 6006: Was the Shutdown Clean?

    6006 is an information record that the Event Log service stopped as part of the shutdown procedure. If 6006 is present and 6008 is absent, the pattern is close to a normal shutdown.

    One event alone does not confirm the power state, though. After a sudden power cut or forced shutdown, 6008 and Kernel-Power 41 may remain without a 6006. Check who requested the shutdown in User32 1074.

    When it appears

    • After a normal shutdown or restart from the Start menu
    • After Windows Update completes a reboot
    • When comparing clean and forced shutdowns in time order

    Likely causes

    • A normal shutdown or restart done by the user
    • A planned reboot by Windows Update or an installer
    • An administrator restarting the Event Log service

    What to check, in order

    1. Compare the 6006 time with the shutdown request time in User32 1074.
    2. Check whether 6005 was recorded again in the same boot cycle.
    3. If only 6008 and Kernel-Power 41 exist without 6006, first check for a power cut or forced shutdown.
    Caution

    6006 itself is not an error. Separate whether the absence of a normal-shutdown record is the problem or whether you actually forced a shutdown.

    Frequently asked questions

    • Is 6006 an error event? No. It is an information record left during a normal shutdown or when the Event Log service stops. The real problem is found in abnormal shutdowns without a 6006 and the events around them.
    • What do I learn from looking at 6006 and 6008 together? 6006 is the record of a normal shutdown process; 6008 is an after-the-fact record that the previous shutdown was not normal. Comparing whether each exists and their times helps separate the shutdown pattern.
